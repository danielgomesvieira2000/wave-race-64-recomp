## Preparation

The supplied MP3 recordings were converted to stereo 48 kHz PCM16 WAV with fixed gain for conversion headroom. The WAV data is untrimmed. `loops.json` supplies playback gain and loop/crossfade settings separately. `manifest.json` records source basenames, source and output SHA-256 hashes, embedded metadata, conversion gain and loop settings. The WAVs and loop configuration match the locally installed assets used for native playback validation.
