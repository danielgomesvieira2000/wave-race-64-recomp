# Wave Race 64: Recompiled 0.9.2

Better water options. The Water tab is now a clear ladder -- **Original,
Enhanced, Best** -- and **Clarity** can take the water from murky to fully
see-through, with the sea floor, the fish and the original game's clear colour
showing beneath the new lighting. The Aqua sliders now visibly do what they say.

**You need your own dump of Wave Race 64 (USA) (Rev A), also called v1.1**
(revision 1, header CRC `0x492F4B61 0x04E5146A`). No other version works. The
launcher checks the file you pick.

## Download

| | |
|---|---|
| **Windows x64** | `WaveRace64Recomp-0.9.2-windows-x64.zip` — unzip, run `WaveRace64Recomp.exe`, pick your dump. Debug symbols are in `…-debug-symbols.zip`, needed only for a readable crash report. |
| **Linux x86-64** | `WaveRace64Recomp-0.9.2-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` — extract, run `./WaveRace64Recomp.sh`. The libraries it needs come from your distribution; `README-LINUX.txt` inside lists them per distro. |
| **macOS (Apple Silicon)** | **No binary in this release.** It builds — `bash tools/setup_macos.sh` then `bash tools/build_macos.sh <your dump>` — but nobody here has a Mac to build or sign one on. |

Settings, saves and mods from 0.9.1 carry over. **One thing will look different:
Aqua brightness, Aqua tint or Clarity saved at anything other than 50%** now has
a much stronger effect, because the old ranges were too weak to see -- set them
again if the water looks off. They live in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\WaveRace64Recomp` on
Windows, `$XDG_DATA_HOME/WaveRace64Recomp` (or `~/.local/share/…`) on Linux and
`~/Library/Application Support/WaveRace64Recomp` on macOS. The log to attach to a
bug report, `wr64.log`, is in the same folder.

Each archive contains the recompiled game code and none of the game's assets;
the executable is a GPL-3.0 combined work (see the README's *Licensing*), and its
source is this repository at tag `v0.9.2`.

## What changed

### A clearer Water tab

| Water quality | |
|---|---|
| **Original** | The game's own water, exactly as it was. Adds no cost. |
| **Enhanced** | New lighting from the sun and sky, colour that deepens with the water, refraction, wakes that linger, wash along the shore. |
| **Best** *(default)* | Enhanced, plus reflections of the scenery and fine spray. |

- **The styles are Classic, Deep and Aqua.** Deep is the richer, darker style
  that used to be called Modern -- which was also the name of a quality level,
  on the same tab.
- **Nothing on the tab is there without doing something.** Under Original the
  options below are greyed out; they come back with Enhanced or Best. Spray is
  greyed out under Enhanced, since it is part of Best. The Aqua sliders only
  appear with Aqua, and Clarity does not appear under Classic, which keeps the
  game's own transparency.
- **Saved settings carry over**: only the names on screen changed.

### Clarity

Now for Deep as well as Aqua. **50% is the usual look.**

- **Above 50%** the water shows what the game draws under the surface -- the sea
  floor, fish, the dolphin -- and comes back toward the original game's clear
  colour, with the new lighting and reflections on top. At 100% it is fully
  see-through. This is the look asked for in
  [#15](https://github.com/danielgomesvieira2000/wave-race-64-recomp/issues/15).
- **Below 50%** the water turns murkier, mostly visible in the shallows.
- **In two-player races only the murkier half applies.** The game draws
  two-player water opaque, so there is nothing clear underneath to show, and
  trying flattened the surface into a pale sheet.

### Aqua brightness and tint

- **Brightness** now runs from well under half as bright to more than twice as
  bright, and **tint** from deep blue to green turquoise. Before, the whole range
  of either changed the picture by about 15% and 5%, which read as a slider that
  does nothing.

None of this costs frame rate: every configuration measured, fully see-through
included, held the game's own 20 frames a second on an Intel Iris Xe laptop.

### For people customising the water

- **The course profiles are documented**, field by field, in
  [WATER.md](../WATER.md#course-profiles): what `authored_reflection` does (it
  is the see-through amount Clarity raises), and how to aim `sun_direction` at a
  course's skybox sun. `WR64_WATER_PROFILES` points the game at an edited copy.
- **`WR64_WATER_CLARITY`, `WR64_WATER_BRIGHTNESS` and `WR64_WATER_TINT`** set
  the sliders for one run, and **`WR64_TEST_OPEN_SETTINGS=water@25`** opens the
  settings on a tab, for capturing a menu.

## Known issues

- **macOS is untested by this project** -- Elliott Tate's work, kept compiling
  here. Treat a macOS-only failure as a real bug and report it.
- **The water renderer has not been run on a Vulkan GPU.** The Linux build was
  exercised on Mesa's software rasteriser, because WSL exposes no GPU.
- **Clarity above 50% fades on very tall swells**, more than about 70 units
  above or below the mean water height.
- **The sun on each course is set by hand** and does not always line up with its
  skybox; the profiles now say how to adjust it.
- **One kind of scenery on Sunset Bay** is drawn to about 5,100 units whatever
  Draw Distance says; its own limit has not been found.
